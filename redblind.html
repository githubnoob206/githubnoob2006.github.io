    Rules & settings

    In bot mode, choose your role and difficulty above the board. The bot locks its move before you choose. Use the original keyboard controls or the on-screen arrows.

    Easy makes frequent mistakes. Normal makes rare mistakes. Hard uses its strongest planning without deliberate mistakes. All bots obey the same movement limits. Timeout is a cop win.

    • Robber: WASD to move one square; Space to stand still. Cop: arrow keys to move two squares in one straight direction. Hold Shift and press an arrow to move just one square, using one of the cop’s single-square moves. Diagonal keys: I ↖, O ↗, K ↙, L ↘. A diagonal move takes two diagonal steps and spends one diagonal use. Shift plus a diagonal key takes one step and spends both a diagonal use and a single-square use. Ctrl plus an arrow or diagonal key takes three steps, spending a three-step use (and a diagonal use if diagonal). All steps must fit inside the board. Do not combine Shift and Ctrl. Set allowances below.
    • Each valid key press locks a secret move. Both players take step one together; then the cop completes any remaining steps, with a pause between hops.
    • The cop wins if both occupy the same square after any step. Moving into a square the robber has left does not count; swapping squares is safe.
    • The robber starts in the center and escapes by moving up from the top row or down from the bottom row into either red strip. Escaping on the final turn still wins.

    The bot plans through the deadline with the default allowances. Custom scarce allowances use a bounded resource-aware search, so those settings are not solved exactly. Apply starts a new game. You can also edit the CONFIG object in this HTML file.
